## What's Possible Now

Market reports. Open houses. Lead questions. Photo swaps. Your website has always been able to do more than publish a blog post. The problem was you couldn't just ask it. Now you can.

When you connect an [MCP server](/technical) (Model Context Protocol — think of it as your website's AI connection) to Claude, you hand an assistant the keys to your website. It can create, edit, delete, and publish content without you logging in manually. That changes what you can delegate and ask for on the spot.

## The 12 Use Cases

### 1. Generate a Market Report Page

**"Create a market report for zip code 94301 and publish it as a new page on my site."**

You ask for a report once per quarter. Instead of writing it yourself or hiring someone to pull data together, the integration creates a page with local sales trends, average prices, and days-on-market. It pulls the real numbers (from public records or your MLS feed), structures them into a page, and publishes it live — all from one sentence.

### 2. Create an Open-House Landing Page

**"Build a landing page for the open house at 123 Main Street, Saturday 2–4 pm, and send me the link."**

An open-house event needs its own destination: directions, parking info, what to expect, a link to your calendar. The integration generates a dedicated page in seconds — optimized for phones, since buyers check it on the walk to the property. You get a shareable link to text or email to your database.

### 3. Draft a Reply to a Lead's Question

**"A lead asked me why this neighborhood is good for families. Draft a reply I can send."**

You paste the lead's message. The integration writes a personalized, professional response that touches on schools, parks, walkability, and community — drawing from your neighborhood guide pages or the property itself. You edit and send it, or send as-is. What used to take 10 minutes becomes 30 seconds.

### 4. Swap Photos on a Listing

**"Replace the kitchen photos on 456 Oak Ave with the new ones I just uploaded."**

The listing has old kitchen shots. You took new ones this morning. Instead of logging into your MLS/website backend, uploading files, and manually swapping them, you just describe what you need. The integration finds the listing, pulls the new images, and makes the swap. It's the same power you'd use to [create or edit a listing](/howto-listing) — but here you're managing photos in bulk.

### 5. Update Your Agent Bio Page

**"I got my CRS designation. Update my bio to mention it and add a link to the CRS website."**

Your biography page is out of date every time you earn a new credential or hit a sales milestone. Ask the integration to insert the new detail in the right place, keep the tone consistent with the rest of your bio, and add the link. No digging through old HTML files.

### 6. Create a Neighborhood Guide Page

**"Create a neighborhood guide for the Oakmont area with schools, restaurants, parks, and transit info. Link to Google Maps for each."**

Neighborhood guides rank well for local search and help buyers see themselves living there. The integration creates a full-page guide: schools ranked by grade, popular restaurants with brief reviews, parks with amenities, transit routes, and links to each. You review it once, publish it, and it lives on your site for months.

### 7. Schedule a Post to Publish Later

**"Write a post about spring home maintenance tips and set it to publish next Tuesday at 10 am."**

You don't have to wake up early to hit your publish schedule. Ask the integration to [publish a blog post](/howto-blog-post) on any topic, then schedule it for whenever you want it live. It drafts, saves, and queues — ready to publish automatically.

### 8. Update SEO Metadata Across Pages

**"Check my listing pages for missing or thin meta descriptions. Fix the ones under 120 characters."**

SEO metadata doesn't write itself. The integration can scan your pages, find ones with weak meta descriptions, and rewrite them to be compelling *and* keyword-smart — all without you manually editing each one.

### 9. Build a Testimonials Page

**"Create a testimonials page featuring the five reviews I sent you in an email. Format them with photos and star ratings."**

You've collected client testimonials. The integration takes them, formats them on a dedicated page with headings, star ratings, and client photos (if you provide them), and publishes it as a new page. Testimonials help buyers trust you faster — and now you can have one live in minutes.

### 10. Create a Comparison Page for Two Listings

**"Build a side-by-side comparison of 123 Main Street and 456 Oak Ave, focusing on price, size, lot, and school districts."**

A buyer is torn between two homes. The integration creates a comparison page: columns for each property, rows for square footage, price, taxes, school ratings, walkability scores, and more. It's the kind of page that closes the sale, and it takes seconds to request.

### 11. Refresh Stale Content

**"My 'About the Area' page is from 2021. Refresh it with current data on schools, new businesses, and recent housing trends."**

Old content drops your SEO ranking and misleads visitors. Ask the integration to update a stale page with fresh numbers, new business openings, school rating changes, and recent market shifts. The structure stays the same; the facts refresh.

### 12. Set Up a Lead-Capture Form

**"Create a form on my site asking for name, email, and what type of property they're looking for. Send submissions to my email."**

You need a way to collect leads from visitors. The integration builds a simple form, places it on a new page or embeds it in an existing one, and wires it up to send responses to your inbox. No coding required.

## Beyond the List

These 12 are just the headline use cases. The real power is that you can ask for almost anything your website might do — and the integration handles the work. Unlike [Zapier](/mcp-vs-zapier), which offers pre-built recipes, an MCP server gives the AI direct access to your site's content. It can create and edit in real-time, understand context, and adapt to what you actually need.

Once you [connect the integration](/connect-claude), you can ask for any of these 12 — or dream up your own.
